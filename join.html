Join ESIL

Build and test the next generation of intelligent energy systems.

ESIL welcomes students who want to connect rigorous engineering models and algorithms with real HVAC, heat-pump, sensor, and building-system data.

Who may be a good fit

Strong applicants do not need to arrive with every skill. We value a solid engineering foundation, evidence of persistence and independent problem-solving, and a genuine interest in energy-system modeling, controls, data, or experimentation.

Thermal & mechanical systems

Thermodynamics, heat transfer, fluid mechanics, HVAC&R, heat pumps, or building energy systems.

Controls & optimization

Dynamic systems, control theory, MPC, system identification, numerical optimization, or cyber-physical systems.

Computation & data

Python/MATLAB, machine learning, time-series analysis, simulation, Modelica, EnergyPlus, or data acquisition.

Prospective Ph.D. students

If you are interested in doctoral research with ESIL, email Dr. Wang with a concise introduction and materials that make it easy to evaluate research fit.

Suggested email package
1) CV or résumé; 2) unofficial transcripts; 3) a short description of your research interests and why they fit ESIL; 4) representative publication, thesis, project, or code sample if available; and 5) your intended application term.

Current OU students
