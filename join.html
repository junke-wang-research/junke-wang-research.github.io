Join ESIL

Build and test the next generation of intelligent energy systems.

ESIL welcomes students who want to connect rigorous engineering models and algorithms with real HVAC, heat-pump, sensor, and building-system data.

Who may be a good fit

Strong applicants do not need to arrive with every skill. We value strong fundamentals, persistence, independent problem-solving, clear communication, and genuine interest in energy-system modeling, controls, data, or experimentation.

Thermal systems

Mechanical & HVAC

Thermodynamics, heat transfer, fluid mechanics, HVAC&R, heat pumps, thermal storage, or building energy systems.

Intelligence

Controls & optimization

Dynamic systems, control theory, MPC, system identification, numerical optimization, or cyber-physical systems.

Computation

Data & simulation

Python/MATLAB, machine learning, time-series analysis, Modelica, EnergyPlus, simulation, or data acquisition.

Ph.D. applicants

Make your research fit easy to understand.

If you are interested in doctoral research with ESIL, email Dr. Wang with a concise introduction and materials that show your preparation and the specific ESIL research direction you want to pursue.

Suggested email package

1) CV or résumé
2) Unofficial transcripts
3) Short statement of research interests and ESIL fit
4) Representative publication, thesis, project, or code sample if available
5) Intended application term
A strong first email

Be specific

Rather than sending a generic request, identify one or two ESIL research directions that genuinely match your background or goals and briefly explain why.

Contact Dr. Wang

Current OU students

Undergraduate and graduate students interested in research are encouraged to email with their major, year, relevant coursework or skills, and a short explanation of the ESIL research area they want to explore.

What students can expect

Projects are intended to connect fundamentals with implementation: mathematical modeling, simulation, data analysis, controller development, instrumentation, experiments, and clear technical communication.
