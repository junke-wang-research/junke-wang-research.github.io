About ESIL

The Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory is led by Dr. Junke Wang in the School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. Our goal is to make energy systems more efficient, adaptive, and grid-responsive while maintaining reliable and comfortable operation.

01

HVAC & Thermal Energy Systems

Heat pumps, vapor-compression systems, water heating, thermal storage, and integrated building energy systems.

02

Advanced Control & Optimization

Model predictive control, supervisory control, real-time optimization, and autonomous operation.

03

Grid-Interactive Buildings

Demand flexibility, load shifting, building-grid integration, and coordinated operation of flexible energy assets.

04

Fault Diagnostics

Fault detection, diagnosis, performance degradation detection, and automated fault correction.

05

Modeling & AI

Physics-based, reduced-order, data-driven, and AI-enabled models for prediction, control, and decision support.

06

Experimental Validation

Controlled-environment testing, instrumentation, laboratory experiments, and field demonstrations of advanced energy technologies.

Selected work

Recent work spans predictive HVAC control, multi-functional heat pumps, building thermal dynamics, demand flexibility, and automated diagnostics.

Heat-pump systems

Modeling, validation, and predictive control of multi-functional vapor-compression systems serving coupled space-conditioning and water-heating loads.

Grid-responsive control

Control-oriented thermal models and predictive strategies for pre-cooling, flexible load operation, and building-grid coordination.

Diagnostics & sensing

IoT-enabled measurements, virtual sensing, fault detection and diagnosis, and data-driven performance monitoring for HVAC systems.
