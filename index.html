Mission

Engineering intelligence that works in real energy systems.

Our mission is to advance the science and engineering of intelligent energy systems to improve efficiency, flexibility, and resilience. We integrate physical understanding, data, intelligent decision-making, and experimental validation to develop solutions that can move from models to real systems.

Research directions

Where we are focusing now

ESIL is building depth in a small number of broad, connected themes that link physical energy systems, intelligent decision-making, flexibility, resilience, and experimental evidence.

Intelligent systems

Adaptive thermal energy systems

Develop models, sensing, and control methods that help HVAC, heat-pump, storage, and coupled thermal systems adapt to changing weather, loads, equipment conditions, and operating objectives.

Autonomy

Safe, resilient, and self-correcting control

Advance predictive, learning, and fault-tolerant control strategies that operate under uncertainty while respecting comfort, equipment, reliability, and safety constraints.

Flexibility

Integrated energy flexibility and building-grid interaction

Coordinate buildings, thermal storage, controllable loads, and distributed energy resources to provide flexible operation across multiple timescales without compromising core building services.

Discovery

Experimentally grounded energy-system intelligence

Use controlled experiments, high-resolution sensing, integrated testbeds, and field data to identify system behavior, validate algorithms, and accelerate learning from real energy systems.
