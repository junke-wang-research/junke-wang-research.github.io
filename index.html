Mission

Engineering intelligence that works in real energy systems.

We study how thermal systems behave, how they can be modeled and diagnosed, and how advanced control can make them more efficient and grid-responsive without sacrificing comfort, reliability, or practical implementability.

Research directions

Where we are focusing now

Thermal systems

Heat pumps, electrification, and multi-service operation

Modeling and control of heat-pump-based systems operating under variable weather, loads, equipment constraints, and coupled thermal services.

Flexibility

Grid-responsive buildings without compromising comfort

Use building thermal dynamics and controllable loads to shift demand, coordinate operation, and respond to grid needs while respecting occupant and equipment constraints.

Reliability

Diagnostics that lead to corrective action

Combine sensing, models, data analytics, and controls so faults are not only detected, but translated into actionable and eventually automated corrections.
