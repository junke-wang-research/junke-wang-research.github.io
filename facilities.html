Facilities

Experimental & computational capabilities

ESIL is developing laboratory infrastructure that supports controlled HVAC testing, instrumentation, data acquisition, advanced controls, and model validation.

01

Controlled-environment testing

An environmental-chamber-based platform is being developed for repeatable HVAC and heat-pump experiments under controlled boundary conditions. Detailed operating ranges and equipment specifications will be added as commissioning is completed.

02

Instrumentation & data acquisition

Sensor and DAQ architectures for temperature, humidity, pressure, airflow, electrical power, equipment state, and control signals support high-resolution system characterization and diagnostics.

03

Modeling & control platforms

Research workflows use MATLAB, Python, Modelica, Simulink, EnergyPlus, OpenStudio, and related tools for dynamic modeling, simulation, optimization, controller development, and data analysis.

Why the facility matters

ESIL is designed around a closed research loop: model → design control/diagnostic methods → implement on hardware → measure performance → update the model. This supports work that is both scientifically rigorous and implementation-oriented.

Facility information is intentionally conservative at launch. Add chamber dimensions, temperature/humidity ranges, HVAC equipment capacity, sensor inventory, photos, and test standards only after each item is verified and commissioned.
