Ph.D. positions available for Spring/Fall 2027

ESIL is actively recruiting doctoral students interested in HVAC and heat-pump systems, advanced control and optimization, grid-interactive buildings, fault diagnostics, modeling/AI, and experimental validation. See application guidance.

The laboratory website now provides research, publications, facilities, people, and recruiting information in one public location.

August 2026

Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory established at OU

Junke Wang joined the School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma as an Assistant Professor and established ESIL.

2025

2025

Building and HVAC model-calibration paper published

Work on automated calibration of coupled residential building and HVAC models using an RC model and genetic-algorithm optimization was published in Building and Environment.

2024

2024

VRF modeling review published in Energy and Buildings

A comprehensive review of modeling variable refrigerant systems in building applications was published in Energy and Buildings.

2024

Smart-thermostat MPC and demand-controlled ventilation studies published

Recent publications addressed model-predictive precooling with smart thermostats and occupancy-sensor-enabled demand-controlled ventilation using virtual outdoor-air flow metering.
