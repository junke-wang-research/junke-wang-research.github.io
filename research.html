Research

Systems, intelligence, and validation.

ESIL combines thermal-fluid science, control and optimization, cyber-physical energy systems, sensing, and data-driven methods to improve the performance, flexibility, and reliability of building and thermal energy systems.

Research thrusts

Each thrust is designed to connect physical understanding with algorithms and measurable performance.

01

HVAC & Thermal Energy Systems

Dynamic behavior, design, and operation of heat pumps, vapor-compression systems, water heating, thermal storage, hydronic systems, and integrated thermal-energy technologies.

02

Advanced Control & Optimization

Model predictive control, supervisory control, control-oriented system identification, constrained optimization, and intelligent or autonomous operation.

03

Grid-Interactive Buildings & Demand Flexibility

Flexible building loads, load shifting, demand response, building-grid coordination, and control of HVAC and distributed energy resources to support grid needs.

04

Fault Detection, Diagnostics & Automated Correction

Detection and isolation of performance degradation, virtual sensing, model-based and data-driven diagnostics, and control actions that restore efficient operation.

05

Physics-Based, Data-Driven & AI-Enabled Modeling

Reduced-order thermal models, digital twins, machine learning, hybrid physics/data models, state and parameter estimation, and predictive analytics for control and decision-making.

06

Experimental Validation & Field Demonstration

Controlled-environment HVAC testing, sensor and data-acquisition development, laboratory controller validation, and performance evaluation in real buildings and energy systems.

Research architecture

Two layers, one objective.

Our application layer focuses on physical thermal systems and flexible buildings. Our intelligence layer provides the models, optimization, controls, diagnostics, and experiments needed to improve them.

Application layer

Thermal systems & flexible buildings

Heat pumps, HVAC equipment, water heating, thermal storage, building thermal dynamics, and grid-interactive operation.

Intelligence layer

Models, controls, diagnostics & experiments

Dynamic modeling, AI and data analytics, optimization, MPC, fault diagnosis, sensing, hardware implementation, and validation.

Workflow

From first principles to measured performance.

Current directions

Near-term ESIL work emphasizes heat-pump controls and performance, demand flexibility, advanced diagnostics, and experimentally validated modeling and control methods.

Heat pumps & electrification

Control and optimization under variable loads, weather, equipment constraints, and multi-service operation.

Controls that transfer to hardware

Methods that can be tested with real sensors, actuators, controllers, and thermal equipment rather than remaining simulation-only.

Flexible & resilient operation

Use building thermal dynamics and controllable loads to provide flexibility while maintaining comfort, efficiency, reliability, and equipment-aware operation.
