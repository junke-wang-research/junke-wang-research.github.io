# Reference sites and what to borrow

## 1. Zheng O'Neill — Building Energy and HVAC&R Research Group, Texas A&M
https://hvac.engr.tamu.edu/

Closest topical benchmark. Strong use of a concise mission, current news, recruiting information, people, facilities/testbeds, and publications. Good model for how ESIL can become more facility-forward as the OU chamber is commissioned.

## 2. Wangda Zuo — Sustainable Buildings and Societies Laboratory, Penn State
https://sites.psu.edu/sbslab/

Strong information architecture. Research is organized into major themes and funded projects; people and publications are easy to browse. Good benchmark for future project pages and alumni tracking.

## 3. Jie Cai — University of Oklahoma AME faculty page
https://www.ou.edu/coe/ame/people/faculty/jie-cai

Useful local reference for concise research-interest vocabulary and the level of detail expected on an OU faculty profile.

## 4. Li Song — Building Energy Efficiency Laboratory, University of Oklahoma
https://www.ou.edu/coe/labs/building-energy-efficiency

Important local benchmark for OU lab structure and institutional presentation. ESIL should remain distinct in research positioning while maintaining compatible OU terminology and navigation expectations.

## 5. Texas A&M Energy Systems Lab — Indoor Environments / Heat Pump Testbed
https://esl.tamu.edu/services/iel/

Useful future benchmark for a facilities page: state the controlled variables, measurable quantities, equipment capacity, and test standards only when verified.

## What not to copy
- Long home-page publication lists.
- Every old project on the main navigation.
- Unverified facility specifications.
- Generic AI language without a direct energy-system application.
- Large “under construction” sections.
