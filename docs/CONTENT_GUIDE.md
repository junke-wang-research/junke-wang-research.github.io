# ESIL Website Content Guide

## Site positioning
**Lab name:** Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory (ESIL)

**Recommended one-line identity:**
> Intelligence for efficient, flexible, and resilient energy systems.

**Recommended short description:**
> ESIL develops intelligent technologies for HVAC and thermal energy systems by integrating physics-based modeling, data-driven methods, optimization, advanced control, sensing, and experimental validation.

## PI biography (website length)
Junke Wang is an Assistant Professor in the School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma and Director of the Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory. His research focuses on modeling, optimization, and control of energy systems, with experience in building thermal dynamics, vapor-compression and multi-functional heat-pump systems, advanced sensing and analytics, fault diagnostics, and grid-interactive building operation. His work integrates thermal-fluid sciences, cyber-physical systems, data-driven methods, and experimental validation to enable efficient, flexible, and resilient energy-system operation. Before joining OU, he conducted research at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory in advanced building controls, heat-pump systems, and high-performance building technologies. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Oklahoma in 2020.

## What to add once available
### Home
- Strong lab photo or chamber/testbed image.
- 2–3 current news items.
- One current recruiting call-to-action.

### Research
For each major project, add:
- Problem / why it matters
- Research question
- Method
- Experimental or simulation platform
- Key outcome
- Sponsor and collaborators
- Related publication(s)

### People
For each student:
- Name, degree program, photo
- 1–2 sentence research topic
- Education
- Optional LinkedIn/Scholar/GitHub

### Facilities
Only publish verified specifications. Useful fields:
- Chamber/test-space dimensions
- Temperature and humidity operating range
- HVAC/heat-pump capacity range
- Airflow measurement range
- Electrical measurement channels
- Temperature/RH/pressure instrumentation
- Data-acquisition and controls hardware
- Controller-in-the-loop / hardware-in-the-loop capabilities

### Join Us
Keep requirements concise. Do not promise funding unless a funded opening is confirmed. Ask for materials that let you assess fit quickly.
