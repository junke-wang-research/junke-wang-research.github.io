# Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory (ESIL)

The **Energy Systems Intelligence Laboratory (ESIL)** is a research group in the School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, led by **Dr. Junke Wang**.

ESIL develops intelligent technologies for HVAC and thermal energy systems through physics-based modeling, data-driven methods, optimization, advanced control, sensing, diagnostics, and experimental validation.

**Live website:** https://junke-wang-research.github.io/

## Research areas

- HVAC & Thermal Energy Systems
- Advanced Control & Optimization
- Grid-Interactive Buildings & Demand Flexibility
- Fault Detection, Diagnostics & Automated Correction
- Physics-Based, Data-Driven & AI-Enabled Modeling
- Experimental Validation & Field Demonstration
