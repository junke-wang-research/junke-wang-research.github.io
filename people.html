JW

Junke Wang, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor
School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering
The University of Oklahoma

Dr. Wang's research centers on modeling, optimization, and control of energy systems, with experience in building thermal dynamics, vapor-compression and multi-functional heat-pump systems, advanced sensing and analytics, fault diagnostics, and grid-interactive building operation. His work integrates thermal-fluid sciences, cyber-physical systems, data-driven methods, and experimental validation.
